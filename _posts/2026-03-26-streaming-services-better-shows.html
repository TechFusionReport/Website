Streaming Services Need Fewer Universes And Better Shows

StreamingTVMar 26, 20263 min read

Bigger franchises do not fix thinner stories. The next streaming win comes from sharper releases and fewer filler seasons.

The strongest entertainment apps help people find the right thing faster. More hubs, universes, and autoplay rows do not automatically make a service better.

What Matters

Discovery, pacing, clear editorial taste, and fewer filler seasons are the real differentiators.
